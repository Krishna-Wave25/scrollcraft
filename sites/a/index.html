Booking house and quarterly journal, est. 2019

A good stay is rarely the biggest room or the longest list of amenities. It is the one where the light falls the right way at four in the afternoon, and someone remembered you take your coffee black.

Small enough to know your name.

No stay on Hollin has more than fourteen rooms. We say so up front, because it changes everything about how a place feels at breakfast.

Casa Alba, Menorca

06:40

Morning is for the terrace.

Light reaches the east wall at 06:40. Breakfast is whatever Marta found at the market: warm ensaimada, tomatoes, a pot of coffee nobody rushes you out of.

Midday belongs to the shade.

The house is built to hold the cool. Six rooms, thick walls, a shallow pool you can reach in four steps. Nothing is scheduled and no one will suggest it.

Dusk is when it earns its price.

The sea goes the colour of the inside of a shell. Everyone ends up at the same long table without being asked, which is the whole idea.

Night is very, very quiet.

No signal worth mentioning, a lamp in every window, and enough stars that guests stop mid-sentence. Sleep is the amenity.

Three rules wehaven’t broken yet.

Most travel sites list everything and let you sort it out. We do the opposite, and it is the only thing we are strict about.

We sleep there first.

An advisor stays at least two nights before a place is listed, in the cheapest room, in the quiet season. Photographs lie. Mattresses don’t.

Fourteen rooms, at most.

Past that, hosts become staff and guests become numbers. We have turned down very beautiful hotels for being one room too large.

The owner picks up the phone.

Before you book, we put you in touch with the person who actually runs the place. If they are not reachable, neither is the booking.
